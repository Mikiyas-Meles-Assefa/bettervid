# TTS competitor pricing/terms (retrieved 2026-10-29)
Cartesia https://cartesia.ai/pricing : Free 20K credits, Pro $5/100K, Startup $49/1.25M, Scale $299/8M. "1 credit equals 1 character", ~750-800 credits per minute. Commercial license from Pro. Overage $65/$45/$38 per 1M credits. Instant clone from Pro.
Cartesia Sonic 3.6 https://docs.cartesia.ai/build-with-cartesia/tts-models/latest : 44 languages incl Arabic (ar); no SSML needed; word timestamps not mentioned on that page.
Gemini https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing : 3.8 Flash TTS standard in $0.50 / out $9.00 per 1M tokens (rises to $1/$18 on 2027-01-01); Flash-Lite out $6 (->$12); Batch half. Audio = 25 tokens/sec. Free tier exists (content used to improve Google products).
Gemini https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/speech-generation : 3.8 Flash TTS 130+ langs incl Egyptian + Standard Arabic; max 2 speakers per request; style via speech_metadata.style; inline <laugh>,<sigh>,<short pause>; 30 prebuilt voices; voice design + replication; timestamps not mentioned.
OpenAI https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing : gpt-4o-mini-tts text in $0.60/1M tok, audio out $12/1M tok; tts-1 $15/1M chars, tts-1-hd $30/1M chars. No newer dedicated TTS listed.
Inworld https://inworld.ai/pricing : Realtime TTS-2 $25/1M chars on-demand (Creator $20); TTS-2 Flash $15 ($10). Instant clone + voice design all tiers; timestamps listed; 200+ langs claimed.
Fish Audio https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/models-pricing/pricing-and-rate-limits : s2.1-pro $15/1M UTF-8 bytes; drama-3 $50 (promo $10 until 2026-11-12); s2.1-pro-free $0 fair use. 1M bytes ~ 12 h speech (vendor). Concurrency 5/15/50. S2 Pro weights: Fish Audio Research License, commercial needs separate license (reported: vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/s2-pro).
MiniMax (reported, openrouter.ai/minimax/speech-2.8-hd): $100/1M chars HD, $60 turbo, clone $1.50/voice. Arabic: partial.

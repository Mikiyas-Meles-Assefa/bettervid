# HyperFrames (HeyGen), OpenMontage
URLs: https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes , https://arceapps.com/blog/hyperframes-vs-remotion-2026/ , https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage
Retrieved: 2026-10-29
- HyperFrames: Apache 2.0, "no per-render fees or commercial-use thresholds", ~74.9k stars, Node 22+ and FFmpeg. HTML + data attributes, seekable GSAP/CSS/Lottie/Three/Anime/WAAPI; "seeks each frame in headless Chrome and encodes with FFmpeg". @hyperframes/aws-lambda for distributed render. ~450 catalog blocks (transitions, titles, kinetic type, captions, charts, maps, 3D, camera moves). 
- Third-party (reported, partly HeyGen-sourced): 5s local render 7-10s vs Remotion 16-20s; first video ~7s vs ~50s.
- OpenMontage: AGPLv3, ~72.8k stars, 11-12 pipelines, uses Remotion and HyperFrames plus FFmpeg, 700+ skill files, budget caps.

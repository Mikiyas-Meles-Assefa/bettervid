# Remotion license and pricing
URLs: https://www.remotion.pro/license , https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq
Retrieved: 2026-10-29
- "free to use for individuals and companies up to three people." Unlimited commercial use.
- Company License "required for collaborations and companies of 4+ people". Headcount of the whole company counts, not users of Remotion.
- Creators: $25/mo per seat. Automators: "$0.01 per render, $100/mo minimum" for batch rendering and automated video products (editors, prompt-to-video apps, Player embedding). Developers on automation projects need no Seat.
- Enterprise: starting at $500/month.
- FAQ: Free License holders may run a SaaS "even if you set up an automation, launch a SaaS". AI-generated Remotion code is allowed. "not allowed to let users bring or upload their own Remotion code."
- Automation = code that calls renderMedia, renderStill, Lambda/Cloud Run/Vercel render fns, CLI render, or <Player>.
- 1 render = one successful video/audio/GIF/still/image sequence. Studio/Player previews not counted.

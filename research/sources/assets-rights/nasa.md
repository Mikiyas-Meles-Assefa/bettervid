# NASA Images API + media guidelines (retrieved 2026-10-29)
Live: https://images-api.nasa.gov/search?q=apollo%2011&media_type=video returns JSON, no key; items have nasa_id, collection.json with video file links.
Guidelines https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ :
- "Most NASA content isn't subject to U.S. copyright and may be used for educational or informational purposes. NASA should be credited as the source."
- Must not "explicitly or implicitly convey NASA's endorsement"; recognizable people may have publicity rights; third-party items are labeled with copyright holder's name and NASA conveys no rights.

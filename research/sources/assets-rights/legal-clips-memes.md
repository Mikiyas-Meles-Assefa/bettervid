# Legal notes (retrieved 2026-10-29)
- Griner v. King for Congress (8th Cir., June 7 2024, Success Kid): campaign fundraising use of meme not fair use; applies Warhol v. Goldsmith (2023). Eric Goldman: noncommercial meme use likely fair use, ads not. https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2024/06/meme-law-alert-meme-use-in-political-ad-isnt-fair-use-griner-v-king.htm
- Giphy ToS (reported): license to GIFs "solely for personal and non-commercial purposes"; Giphy cannot clear underlying studio rights. Tenor ToS stricter. https://legalclarity.org/are-gifs-copyrighted-the-legal-rules-for-use/
- YouTube Content ID: cannot judge fair use; claim blocks monetization even for few seconds; EFF: 98 percent of claims automatic. https://www.eff.org/wp/unfiltered-how-youtubes-content-id-discourages-fair-use-and-dictates-what-we-see-online ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148
- youtube-dl RIAA 2020 sec 1201 takedown, reversed by GitHub; no US court ruling on yt-dlp; downloading breaches YouTube ToS (contract). https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2020/11/github-reinstates-youtube-dl-after-riaas-abuse-dmca ; Yout lost in D. Conn. https://torrentfreak.com/yout-seeks-clarification-on-the-legality-of-youtube-dl-based-software-221212/
- YouTube Data API search.list videoLicense=creativeCommon (requires type=video); CC option = CC BY; verify with videos.list status.license. https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/search/list
- Archival rates: news footage ~$80-150/sec with 30s minimum; Producers Library $49-79/sec; BFI GBP3,600 first 60s. https://documentarycameras.com/how-to-license-television-news-footage/ ; E&O insurers want clearance log and fair use opinion letters: https://www.dfirentals.com/news/archival-footage-licensing-cost

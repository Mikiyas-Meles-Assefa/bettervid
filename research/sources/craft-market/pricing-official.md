# Competitor pricing, official pages, fetched 2026-10-29 [verified unless noted]
## invideo https://invideo.io/pricing
Starter $20/mo monthly ($10 annual) 400 credits/seat, Agent Two Lite only, no Seedance 2.5. Plus $60 ($30 annual) 2,000 credits (~52 Seedance 2 videos), all models. Max $150 ($75 annual) 5,000 credits (~131 Seedance 2 videos). "Unused credits don't roll over". Seedance 2.5 1080p ~96 credits per 5 s. NOTE: third-party reviews from Aug-Sep 2026 list an older Plus $17/Max $85/Generative $170/Elite $900 structure; page changed.
## HeyGen https://www.heygen.com/pricing
Free $0 (3 videos/mo, watermarked, up to 1 min, Avatar IV and Video Agent). Creator $29/mo ($24 annual) 600 credits, Video Agent expanded. Pro $49 1,000 credits, Video Agent max access, 4K, 30 min. Business $149 1,500 credits. "Add team members for $20/seat/month". Guide: https://www.heygen.com/blog/video-agent-prompt-guide ("Video Agent isn't magic"; "sometimes the visuals can look a bit off from scene to scene").
## Synthesia https://www.synthesia.io/pricing
Free 500 credits/mo, 10 min, watermark. Starter $19/mo ($14 annual billed $168) 1,250 credits (~12 min video). Pro $89/mo ($59 annual) 6,000 credits (~60 min). "Video: 100 credits per minute". Enterprise custom.
## OpusClip https://www.opus.pro/pricing
Free (watermark), Starter $15/mo, Pro $29/mo, Business custom. Pro has "Prompt to clip", MCP connector, API.
## Pictory https://pictory.ai/pricing
Starter $29 monthly ($25 annual) 30 min/mo monthly; Professional $59 ($35 annual) 90 min/mo; Teams $199 ($119 annual) 300 min/mo. Has "AI video agent". 14-day trial.
## Descript https://www.descript.com/pricing
Free; Hobbyist $16 annual/$24 monthly (10 media hours, 400 AI credits); Creator $24/$35 (30 h, 800 credits); Business $50/$65.
## Revid https://www.revid.ai/pricing
Growth $39/mo (2,000 credits, Seedance 2.5, auto-mode workers, API/MCP/CLI, "3M+ viral videos to remix"); Ultra $199/mo (12k credits). Credits do not roll over. Boost packs 1,000 credits for $49.

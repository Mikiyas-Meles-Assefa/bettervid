# Kurzgesagt process
URLs: https://medium.com/@Kurzgesagt/how-research-and-factchecking-work-at-kurzgesagt-f5b239188255 ; https://www.forbes.com/sites/danidiplacido/2024/10/24/kurzgesagt-in-a-nutshell-creator-talks-youtube-tiktok-and-optimistic-nihilism/ ; their video "How to Make a Kurzgesagt Video in 1200 Hours" (transcripts on glasp/lingq)
Retrieved: 2026-10-29 (via search summary, reported)
- 1,200+ hours per video. Stages: topic, research, script, storyboard, illustration, narration, animation, sound/music.
- Writing is the bottleneck; scripts take weeks to years. Experts fact-check.
- ~200 illustrated panels per video; 2-3 illustrators, 8-12 weeks.
- Narration recorded AFTER visuals are illustrated.
- Dettmer (Forbes 2024): "a fine line between over-simplifying and making things overly-complicated". Short-form needed "completely rethink[ing] our processes... It took us a year".

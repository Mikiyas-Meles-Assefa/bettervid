# MrBeast leaked production guide (summary by Daniel Scrivner)

Excerpts:
- "The first minute of each video is the most important minute of each video."
- "we lost 21 million viewers in the first minute of the video"
- Min 1-3: "Stop telling people what they will be watching and start showing them."
- ~Min 3: re-engagement spectacle. Min 3-6: "lots of quick scene changes and highly stimulating simple content that reflects the story". ~Min 6: second re-engagement.
- "Don't ever signal the end of the video unless it's to build hype for the prize or payoff"
- "The video endings must always be abrupt to protect retention."
- "No dull moments in videos"; "You can't fake intensity in videos"; "BRAND DEALS ARE CONTENT!"
- "for 50 million people to understand something it must be simple."
- Title/thumbnail promise must be delivered exactly (yellow castle cannot become red).
- "In stair stepping, As you get deeper in the video the stakes get higher."
- Metrics: CTR, AVD, AVP.

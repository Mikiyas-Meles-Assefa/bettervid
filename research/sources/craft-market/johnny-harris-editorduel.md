# Johnny Harris retention mechanics (third-party analysis, vendor blog)
URL: https://blog.editorduel.com/blog/johnny-harris-high-retention-documentary-content
Retrieved: 2026-10-29. Confidence: reported (secondary, tool-vendor blog).
- Hook montage clips "less than a second" each; explanatory segments "one to two seconds per shot".
- "By second six, the viewer is already committed."
- Hook "must create a knowledge gap that only the rest of the video can close."
- Question-to-Report loop: bold on-screen question, report answers it, raises a new question. "Withhold, tease, resolve." "Each answer is a cliffhanger."
- B-roll "not as filler but as narrative punctuation" (archival, maps, behind-the-scenes).
- Color grade "consistently desaturated and cinematic". SFX: door creaks, paper rustle, typing.
Also (Motion Array / motionvid, reported): stepped 12fps map annotations; place names appear when narrator says them; paper-grain maps; match cut sequences ~8 frames each with camera click SFX.

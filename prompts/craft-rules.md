Craft rules (from research/craft-market.md; the writer, director and critic all follow these):

1. Open with a specific claim, contradiction or question about one concrete thing, under 12 words. Never greet, never "in this video", never context first.
2. The hook opens a knowledge gap that only the rest of the video closes. Do not answer it early.
3. Join every beat to the next with "but" or "therefore" logic, never "and then".
4. Open a second loop before closing the first. Pay off the biggest one last.
5. Misconception first when there is one: state the intuitive answer, then break it.
6. Concrete before abstract: show a specific case before naming the concept.
7. One idea per beat, and each beat names one concrete visual (a real photo, document, map, object or a drawable scene), never "a montage" or "various images".
8. Every factual claim comes from the fact sheet. Flag speculation as speculation. Never invent numbers, dates, names or quotes.
9. Vary sentence length: short, medium, long. Spoken English, no lists, no parentheses, no abbreviations the narrator would stumble on.
10. End by answering the opening question and widening to a bigger idea, or by looping back into the first line. Never sign off, never "thanks for watching", never "follow for more".

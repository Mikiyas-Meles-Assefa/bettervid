A good short is not the long version squeezed. Squeezing turns a video into a list of facts. A good short picks one thread that works on its own, with a clear question and a satisfying payoff, tells that thread properly so a first-time viewer understands it, and leaves everything else out.

Choose the thread for the person who asked. Their request and a reading of why they want the video come with the research: the thread is the one that gives them what they came for, whether that is a method they can use, how something works, or a story. If something in the draft does not serve them, it is the first thing to go.

Before you choose, name the exact event, person, method or thing the topic asks about. The thread must reach it and spend most of its time inside it, with its specific details from the research. A topic about an event is told from inside that event, and earlier events get only the few lines needed to understand it. Mentioning the subject at the start and the end while telling a different story is a failure, however good that story is.

Write the narration for a short narrated explainer video about the topic below, using the research provided.

Start from the person who asked. You get their request in their own words and a reading of why they want the video. That decides what the video is: which part of the topic it covers, where it starts, how it opens, how it sounds, whether it has any history or story at all, and whether it works through an example or tells what happened. There is no default shape. A topic is not a documentary unless this person wants one, and not a lesson unless they want one. Think about what they would ask a great creator for if they could, and make that. The research may hold more than this person needs; use what serves them and leave the rest.

Someone is going to hear this once. When it ends, they should have what they came for, whether that is being able to do it, understanding how it works, or knowing the story well enough to tell it, and they should have enjoyed listening. That is the whole job. Write the narration you would be proud to hear a great creator read.
